## Executive Summary

The AI market in December 2025 is characterized by **unprecedented infrastructure investment**, **fierce competitive dynamics**, and **accelerating compute demands**. Project Stargate represents the largest AI infrastructure commitment in history ($500B), while the competitive landscape shows surprising shifts with Anthropic capturing enterprise market share from OpenAI.

**Key Findings**:
- 🚀 **Project Stargate**: $500B investment over 4 years, 10 GW capacity target
- 💰 **AI VC Funding**: $170B+ in 2024-2025, 33% of all global VC
- 🏆 **Market Leadership Shift**: Anthropic surging in enterprise (32% share vs OpenAI's 27%)
- 💻 **Compute Demand**: NVIDIA GB200 sold out through 2025, 30x performance over H100
- ⚡ **Power Constraint**: Validated as PRIMARY bottleneck for AI infrastructure expansion

**Peru 2040 Implications**: The massive infrastructure buildout and power constraints create a **STRONGER OPPORTUNITY** for Peru's energy-abundant AI strategy, but the competitive window is **2-3 years** as major players lock in capacity.

**Lesson for Peru 2040**: Even Microsoft's massive resources are insufficient for OpenAI's needs. Confirms that **speed and scale of energy/compute availability** are competitive differentiators.

## 5. Edge Computing & Distributed AI Infrastructure

**Note**: Web search tool was unavailable for detailed edge computing data. Analysis based on general knowledge and limited available information.

### General Edge AI Trends

**Deployment Drivers**:
1. **Latency Requirements**: Real-time applications need on-device/edge processing
2. **Privacy/Security**: Data sovereignty and compliance driving local processing
3. **Bandwidth Costs**: Reducing cloud transmission costs for high-volume applications
4. **Offline Operation**: Industrial, automotive, remote applications need local capability

**Market Segments**:
- **Mobile Devices**: On-device AI chips in smartphones (Apple Neural Engine, Google Tensor, Qualcomm)
- **Industrial IoT**: Factory automation, predictive maintenance, quality control
- **Automotive**: Autonomous vehicles, ADAS requiring real-time edge processing
- **Retail**: Smart cameras, checkout-free stores, inventory management
- **Healthcare**: Medical devices, remote monitoring, diagnostics

### Cloud vs Edge Trade-offs

**Cloud Advantages**:
- Massive compute for training and complex inference
- Centralized model updates and management
- Cost efficiency for batch processing
- Easier scaling for variable loads

**Edge Advantages**:
- Ultra-low latency (sub-100ms requirements)
- Data privacy and compliance
- Reduced bandwidth costs
- Offline/intermittent connectivity support

**Market Evolution**:
- Hybrid architectures emerging (training in cloud, inference at edge)
- Model compression techniques (quantization, pruning) enabling edge deployment
- Specialized edge AI chips proliferating (NPUs, TPUs, edge accelerators)

### Implications for Peru 2040

**Differentiated Positioning**:
1. **Cloud-First Strategy**: Peru's energy advantage suited for centralized, high-power AI
2. **Not Edge-Focused**: Edge requires semiconductor manufacturing (difficult for Peru)
3. **Regional Cloud Hub**: Position as Latin America's AI cloud infrastructure
4. **Hybrid Support**: Cloud training + edge deployment model for regional customers
